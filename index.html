About

The next advantage is not a better tool. It is a better system.

I am an AI-forward technology product strategist and business architecture professional with more than 20 years of experience across telecommunications BSS/CCB, B2B SaaS, billing, customer management, provisioning, analytics, and enterprise technology.

I believe the next competitive advantage will come from building supercompanies: organizations in which people, products, data, workflows, and AI systems work together to create dramatically more value than traditional operating models allow.

I see AI not as a side experiment or simple productivity tool, but as a new way to design products, make decisions, serve customers, and scale expertise.
